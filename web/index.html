Board games against an AI that taught itself

Two board games you can play free in your browser. Each computer opponent was trained with self-play reinforcement learning in the style of AlphaZero: a neural network and Monte Carlo tree search that learned only from games against itself. The AI runs on your own device; nothing is sent to a server.

বাঘ-ছাগল (Bagh-Chagol): the tiger and goats game

A traditional hunt game from rural Bengal. The tiger captures goats by jumping over them; the goats win by trapping the tiger. Play either side on four boards, including the local 13-point hourglass board.

Four in a Row

Drop discs into a seven-column board and be the first to line up four, across, down or diagonally. Pick a KAPLAY crew member for your discs and take on Bobo, Ghosty or Gigagantrum.
