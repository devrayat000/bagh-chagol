বাঘ-ছাগল (Bagh-Chagol): the tiger and goats board game

Bagh-Chagol is a traditional hunt game from rural Bengal. One side plays the tiger, the other a herd of goats. The tiger moves along the lines of the board and captures a goat by jumping over it onto the empty point behind. The goats cannot capture: they win by surrounding the tiger so it has no legal move.

Play against an AI that taught itself

The computer opponent was trained with self-play reinforcement learning in the style of AlphaZero: a neural network and Monte Carlo tree search, learning only from games against itself. It runs entirely in your browser. Choose easy, medium or hard.

Boards

  • Hourglass: the local 13-point board with 1 tiger and 7 goats; goats move first.
  • Little Square: a 3 by 3 warm-up board with 1 tiger and 3 goats.
  • Village Square: the classic 5 by 5 alquerque board with 1 tiger and 8 goats.
  • Four Tigers: the 5 by 5 board with a tiger in every corner and 12 goats.
