Four in a Row: play free against a self-taught AI

Take turns dropping discs into a board seven columns wide and six rows high. Each disc falls to the lowest free hole in its column. The first player to line up four of their discs, across, up and down or diagonally, wins. If the board fills up with no line, the game is a draw. Red always moves first.

An AI that learned by playing itself

The computer opponent was trained with self-play reinforcement learning in the style of AlphaZero: a neural network and Monte Carlo tree search that learned only from games against itself. It runs entirely in your browser. Choose easy (Bobo), medium (Ghosty) or hard (Gigagantrum), and go first or second.

Pick your crew

Your discs carry a character from the KAPLAY crew: Bean, Mark, Kat, Ghosty, Bobo, Marroc, Onion or Zombean. Undo a move, share your win with friends, and play again.
